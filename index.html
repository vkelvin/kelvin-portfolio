MINDANAO RADIO

This Is Where You Belong.

About Us

Mindanao radio?

Mindanao Radio is a community station located in Kitale but Broadcasting across the globe

Our mission

Our mission is to give the youth a place where they belong. For this reason we are set on making sure the youth are heard and that their voice matters.

Our Vision

To offer world class entertainment in music by playing requested tracks and top notch new and classical Hits.

Our Services

  • Facilitating dissemination of critical information to the community.

  • Entertaining our audiences via our dynamic music playlists.

  • Voicing out concerns of the society to those with the capacity to help

  • We support outreach programs in the society whose efforts are geared towards education and community health

  • Product promotion (trade and investment in the society)

  • Providing a platform for the nurturing and discovery of talents in the community
